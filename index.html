01 / case studyLive project

Messy inbox in. Real work out.

Python Streamlit SQLite LLM API

Customer messages arrive unstructured — different wording, tone, and detail every time. Someone has to read each one, work out what the customer needs, judge how urgent it is, and decide what happens next, before any real work starts.

This tool does that first pass: it sorts the message, ranks its urgency, routes it to a department, and drafts a reply — then hands the whole thing to a person for a yes or no.

  • The AI drafts, a human decides. Every field is editable and nothing is final until someone approves it. The model is explicitly barred from promising refunds, replacements, or delivery dates — those are business decisions, not classification tasks.
  • Model output is never trusted directly. A validation layer checks every field against approved values before anything is stored, so an unexpected response becomes a safe default rather than a bad record.
  • The AI provider is configuration, not code. Swapping the underlying model is an environment variable, not a rewrite.

02 / case studyIn practice

Know what to design next.

AI Workflow Market Intelligence Trend Research Human-in-the-Loop

Built for my own digital-design business: a repeatable research workflow that reads Etsy, Pinterest, Faire, and Creative Fabrica for market evidence, emerging visual signals, boutique & wholesale taste, and creator-supply saturation — then turns them into structured, scored product opportunities.

A trend on one marketplace is interesting. The same movement appearing independently across multiple sources is far more meaningful. Each opportunity is scored on demand, momentum, repeatability, customization, boutique fit, and competition gap, then classified as Make Now, Next, Watch, or Saturated — a decision-support system, not a guarantee of sales.

  • AI assists the research. Tagging, clustering, and summarizing evidence across sources — not making the creative call.
  • The system structures it. Every signal is normalized and scored against the same criteria, so patterns are comparable.
  • I decide. Taste, originality, and whether a trend is worth pursuing stay a human judgment — the same principle behind everything else I build: AI drafts, systems validate, people decide.
